Estimated gratuity Eligible service:

Gratuity is generally calculated at 15 days' wages for each completed year of service, with service of more than six months counted as an additional year. Rules may vary for fixed-term employees and other special cases.

GUIDE

How Gratuity is Calculated in India

Gratuity is a lump-sum benefit paid by an employer to an eligible employee when employment ends under applicable conditions.

Gratuity Formula

Gratuity = Monthly wages × 15 ÷ 26 × completed years

A period of more than six months is generally counted as an additional completed year for the standard calculation.

Example

If monthly wages considered for gratuity are ₹1,00,000 and the employee has completed 5 years and 6 months:

₹1,00,000 × 15 ÷ 26 × 6 = ₹3,46,154

This calculator provides an estimate. Actual gratuity depends on the employee's circumstances, applicable statutory provisions and employer records.
